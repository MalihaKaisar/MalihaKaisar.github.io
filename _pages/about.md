I'm a Research Intern (remote) at Elite Research Lab LLC, working on AI, machine learning, and computer vision, collaborating remotely with an international research team. My research centers on large language models, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and natural language processing, with a particular focus on Bangla NLP. My interests also include LLM reasoning and AI deception detection, building on earlier work in stylometry and authorship attribution.

I hold a BSc in Computer Science & Engineering from Sylhet Engineering College (affiliated with Shahjalal University of Science and Technology), Bangladesh.

Research
======
My current work spans:
* **Bangla NLP** — building resources and benchmarks for a comparatively under-served language
* **LLM reasoning** — how large language models handle structured and unstructured reasoning tasks
* **AI deception detection** — distinguishing AI-generated deceptive text from human-written deception
* **Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)** — applying and evaluating RAG pipelines for information retrieval
* **Stylometry & authorship attribution** — my earlier research focus, now feeding into my deception-detection work

I have three peer-reviewed IEEE publications — ICCIT 2025, IEEE SPICSCON 2025, and ICCCNT 2025 (IIT Indore) — with two further manuscripts currently in preparation. See my [Publications](/publications/) page for details.

I'm currently applying for fully funded direct-entry PhD positions (BSc to PhD) at Canadian universities for Fall 2027.
